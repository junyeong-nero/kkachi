# DESIGN.md: Kkachi web app

Single source of truth for the visual system of Kkachi Home (까치 홈, the app on the user's phone: `src/web/index.html`) and of the `/device` page. Interaction design (voice flow, states, latency) lives in [docs/design.md](docs/design.md).

## Discovery

- **Artifact**: mobile AI/conversational interface, text-first with optional dictation and read-aloud (Operate mode). Used one-handed on a phone.
- **Who / job**: one person talking to their own assistant. Primary action: type (or dictate) something, check it, send.
- **Brief**: clean and neutral like other AI products; the magpie (까치) appears only as the app icon and logo, the way Kitsune uses its fox. An earlier full-screen magpie illustration was rejected as kitschy.
- **Adjectives**: calm, clear, quick, trustworthy. Essence: *quiet text assistant*.

## Commitments

- **Aesthetic**: plain neutral surfaces, one accent, content first. Familiar chat patterns (your words right, Kkachi's answer left) because Operate mode rewards familiarity.
- **Signature move**: the mic button that draws your live voice level as a halo while recording, and "remembered" chips in the same magpie-wing teal. Teal only ever means "Kkachi is listening" or "Kkachi remembered this".
- **Symbol**: flat magpie head-and-shoulders mark (black, white belly and wing, one teal wing patch) on pale mint `#CEE8E2`, generated with Codex image generation on deep ink `#16181C` and moved onto mint by `assets/icon/recolor.py` (the black bird disappeared on the ink at home-screen size). Used for the app icon and the header logo only. Source and the two unused candidates (perched profile on off-white, in flight on pale teal) are in `assets/icon/`; `src/web/icon.png` (512), `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `favicon.png` (64) are resized from `icon-b-mint.png`.

- **Type**: SUIT Variable (Korean-native sans; not Inter/Pretendard by reflex). Scale ratio 1.2 from 16px: 13 / 16 / 19 / 23. Korean line breaks with `word-break: keep-all`.
- **Radius**: 8px (chips, small controls), 14px (bubbles, groups, sheet), pill (buttons, mic).
- **Shadow**: one soft elevation `0 1px 2px` in light mode only; dark mode uses lightness steps.
- **Motion**: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`, 120ms / 200ms. Only transform, opacity, color. Reduced motion keeps color and opacity changes, drops movement. The voice-level halo is a disc behind the mic scaled with transform, never an animated box-shadow, so focus rings appear at once.
- **What moves** (Kkachi Home; asked for, and added with impeccable's animate and the deslop motion reference): motion says where something came from or that a tap was taken, and nothing plays when the app opens. Reduced motion keeps every fade and drops every movement.
  - The bar's pill is one thing that slides from tab to tab (260ms); the screen arrived at fades in (200ms). A view opened in place (a device, 새 연결) also comes 12px from the right, and going back from the left (260ms).
  - A switch moves where it is, its knob widening while held, and its row dims after it (200ms); the list is drawn again only once it has moved. Before, the list was redrawn at once and the switch was never seen to move.
  - 연결's focal moment: a connection that was not there before has its wire drawn, down the trunk and across to the arrowhead (about half a second), then the place fades in. The dot that runs along a wire still means only that something went through.
  - 편집: the switches and the bins change places one row after another (20ms apart, 120ms at most; from 80% and clear, 200ms). A removed connection fades where it is (150ms; the last one takes its group) and what is left slides up into its place (260ms).
  - The toast comes 8px down from the top edge as it fades in. The `/device` page is left as it was: it sits lit in a room all day.
- **Spacing**: mostly 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20 and 24px, with 3 to 9px insets inside dense controls (chips, segments, table cells). Hallmark's 4px scale is noted, not adopted: the 10 and 14px values set the bubble and card padding, and changing them is a layout change, not a polish.

## Craft decisions

- **Screens**: a bar floating over the bottom of the screen switches five screens: 입력, 출력, 연결, 에이전트, 설정. It is a translucent pill, asked for in the style of Apple's floating tab bars: `bg` at 72% over a 20px backdrop blur, at most 400px wide, 12px above the bottom edge (or the home-indicator inset), lifted by a two-layer shadow in light mode and a hairline in dark mode. The screens scroll behind it and end with room to clear it. Each tab is a 22px line icon over a 12px label, `muted`; the current one is `text` and bold on a 9% `text` pill (no teal: it is not listening). 연결 opens first. The bar goes away while the on-screen keyboard is up. There is no header: the screens start under the status bar, and the logo is the app icon only. Refresh is an icon button at the right end of the settings screen's heading. Nothing is a popup: settings is a screen, and a device or a new connection opens in place with a `muted` back button ("‹ 입력") above its heading; tapping the current tab again also goes back. Every screen starts with a 23px heading and at most one `muted` paragraph; no label sits above a heading.
- **Tiles**: 입력 and 출력 are grids (columns of at least 148px, 10px gaps) of `surface` tiles, radius 14px, one per spare device (this app is not among them: it is where they are looked after, and no row or tile stands for it): at the top a 36px round `bg` picture of what the device is (a line icon: a phone, a tablet, and a bulb or a wall switch once such devices are joined), then the name, and a `muted` line ("6개 쓸 수 있어요", 꺼져 있어요, 허용을 기다려요). A device that is off or not allowed has its name `muted` and regular weight. A tile darkens to `line` on hover and press and shrinks to 98% while pressed. A tile opens that device: its state as a sentence, then grouped rows, one per thing it can send (입력) or take (출력), each its 36px round picture (the same line icons as on `/device`), a title and a light. Titles only, named for the thing (음성, 다이얼, 버튼, 카메라, 소리 크기, 가속도 센서, 위치; 스피커, 조명): no description under them, and no paragraph under the 입력 and 출력 headings.
- **Lights**: whether something can be used is one 10px dot, the same everywhere: `ok` green it can, `danger` red it cannot (no permission, not supported, needs a tap on its screen, or a device waiting to be allowed), grey (`muted` at 45%) the device is off. A row's light is at its right end, a tile's at its top right; under a device's rows one `muted` line names the three. The words stay for screen readers (the dot's label). Green and red here are states, not the accent: teal still means only listening and remembered. A device waiting to be allowed has a filled 이 기기 허용 button there.
- **Edges**: each side of a screen has two lines. The screen's own, 20px in: a heading starts on it, a group fills from one to the other, and what is drawn in the icon button at the end of a heading (the plus, refresh) stops on it, the button itself running 14px past. A group's own, 14px inside its surface: a row's picture or first word starts on it, and the control that ends a row stops on it, the same on every screen (a switch's track on 연결 and 자원, the remove pill on 연결, a 삭제 or 허용 pill on 설정; the light of a row on 입력 and 출력 is not a control and sits 4px further in). A control is at least 44px to tap and that area may run past the line: it is what is seen that lines up. Before, the same switch ended 7px in on 연결 and 17px in on 자원, and the cross that removed a connection about 20px in. Checked against Material 3 (Lists: one position for each part of a row, controls in the trailing slot, 16dp before it; Switch: it acts at once and sits beside its label), Apple's guidelines (a switch belongs in a list row, whose words are its label; an edit mode for removing) and WCAG 2.2 (target size). The rules are taken from them, not the sizes: the switch (38 by 22px) and the 14px inset are this app's own, and the 44px target is Apple's (Material asks for 48dp).
- **Connections (연결)**: the heading with 편집 and a plus at its right end, then a routing table grouped by input: where each input goes, which is the agent (a voice only; shown as 에이전트 with the same line icon as its tab, a name and 대화 under it, never as 까치 or the magpie mark: the screens about wiring say what the thing is, and every end looks alike) or an output of another device. What the agent may use is not here: it is 자원 on the agent screen. Nothing else (a greeting and a line about the agent were tried here and removed, and so were cards that showed input, agent and output as one chain: an input goes to the agent or to an output, and where the agent answers is a setting). These three screens are read again every 5 seconds while showing, and redrawn only when something on them changed.
  - **Signature here: the wire is the structure.** One `surface` group per input, because one input can go to several places. Its first line is the input, written once: a bare 20px line icon, the device in 700 and what it is in `muted` ("자취방 음성"). Under it, every place it goes to, hung on a 1.5px `muted` wire that comes down from the input's icon and branches to each with an arrowhead; the trunk stops at the last place. A place is a bare icon, a name ("에이전트", "주방 스피커", "주방 조명 밝기"), how it is doing in `muted` 13px under it, and a switch at the end of the row, 16px clear of the words (see Edges). No round badges and no rules inside a group: the wire and the indent carry the grouping. This replaced a stack of equal cards with a divider and three controls each (reviewed with the impeccable and taste skills: same-size cards as the page structure, a badge on every end, and no way to see one input going to two places).
  - A place that is switched off goes `muted` at the weight it had, so its name does not wrap anew when its switch is flipped; an input that is off goes `muted` and regular weight; a switched-off branch fades to 45%.
  - Every place is built the same, the agent too. **Live**: when something goes through, a 7px `text` dot runs along that branch once (520ms, ease-out; `danger` when it could not be delivered; with reduced motion it shows in the middle and fades) and the words under the name change at once: "마지막 값 70", "오후 1:02에 눌렀어요", "오후 1:02에 들었어요", 값을 기다려요, 꺼 두었어요, 받는 기기가 꺼져 있어요, or 전달하지 못했어요 in `danger`.
  - 편집 (a `muted` text button, `text` while on, then 완료) puts a remove pill where each switch was: the pill of 설정's device list holding an 18px bin in `danger` in place of a word (asked for: an icon, not 삭제), 38 by 32px, so it starts and ends where the switch's track did and nothing else in the row moves. Its name for screen readers is the connection and 삭제. It asks first and names the connection ("자취방 음성에서 주방 스피커로 가는 연결을 삭제할까요?"). Removing is rare, so it does not sit on every row, and a row with a switch has no second action. A `danger` cross was there before: it read as closing, or as an error beside 전달하지 못했어요, which a bin does not.
  - The plus beside 편집 is 새 연결. It opens two questions in place, each a grid of tiles: 어떤 입력에서 시작할까요? (the chosen tile gets a 2px `text` outline), then 어디로 보낼까요?, each lamp (tablets only) for a dial or a button, and for a voice 에이전트 (when not joined yet) and each other device's speaker; choosing one makes the link and returns to the rows. With no dial switched on anywhere, a note says how to get one.
- **Agent screen**: a four-part segmented control at the top: 대화, 기록, 자원, MCP. 자원 is the outputs the agent may use, under the heading 에이전트가 쓰는 출력: for each device its name as a small `muted` heading and a grouped list, one row per output (스피커, and 조명 on a tablet: a phone is never a lamp, because a lit lamp is the whole screen and took the touches meant for its pads) with its round picture, its title and a switch (track `text` when open). This is the only place where an output is opened to the agent or closed; under the devices, 공기계 대화 (how it answers what comes from a device) with its segmented controls. 대화 is the transcript and composer below, the composer sitting on the bar at the bottom. 기록 lists the last 30 turns from Kkachi Home and the spare devices: the words (two lines at most), then when, where from and how it went as sentences ("오전 11:04, 까치 홈. 6.8초 걸렸어요. 도구를 1번 썼어요"), a failed one in `danger`. MCP lists 까치 서버 (the servers in `kkachi.mcp.json`, each "연결됨. 도구 N개" or "응답이 없어요" in `danger`) and 내 Pi 서버 (names registered in the user's Pi, "등록됨": Pi connects to them itself, so no live state).
- **Conversation layout**: transcript / composer: `(photo) [text box] (mic) (send)`, with a one-line status above it only when there is something to say (recording, transcribing, an error). Picked or taken photos (up to 4, scaled to 1600px JPEG on the phone) wait as 64px thumbnails with a remove button above the text box, and appear above your words in your bubble once sent. A photo alone can be sent. The mic is tap to start / tap to stop, or hold and let go; transcribed words are appended to the text box to check and fix, and nothing is sent until you press send (or Enter).
- **Transcript**: last 30 turns of this page, as chat bubbles: yours a light `surface` fill on the right, Kkachi's outlined (`bg` with a `line` border) on the left (fill vs outline keeps them apart without heavy color) with the working dots and activity label inside it. Kkachi's bubble appears once your words are known and is removed if the turn ends without words. Replies are Markdown (marked + DOMPurify, vendored in `src/web/vendor/`); narration before a tool call stays in the bubble as a muted aside. Read-aloud is off by default (per device) and speaks only the first two sentences.
- **Settings**: a screen of grouped rows and segmented controls under small `muted` headings, the same lists as 기록 and MCP.
- **Devices**: a settings section lists spare phones and tablets joined at `/device` (name, then 허용 대기 / 꺼져 있음 / 연결됨 with what it can do now) with 허용 or 삭제.
- **`/device` page**: dark only, because it sits in a room all day. The main screen is for talking to Kkachi; the switches are in a settings sheet, and the lamp has no controls here (Kkachi picks its colour). The page always fits the screen and never scrolls: the button scales with the height (80 to 136px) and heard and said are cut to a few lines. The talk button sits at the exact centre of the screen: top and bottom padding are equal, and the rows above and below it are equal.
  - a floating bar with the logo and the device name (22px), and a settings icon button on the right
  - at the centre, the device's own inputs as square pads in two columns (12px gap, radius 14px, `surface` with a `line` border, each 96 to 164px by the screen), one for each that is switched on in settings: 음성, 버튼, 다이얼. One pad alone sits in the middle; a third goes under the first. Each pad has its line icon above a 13px `muted` title. The button sits at the exact centre only when there is one row
  - above the button, what was heard in quotes (`muted`) and the words spoken (current ones in `text`, earlier ones `muted`), sitting just over it
  - below the pads, one status line; at the bottom, the input environment: a pill with one icon per input (음성, 카메라, 소리 크기, 가속도 센서, 위치), dimmed when off or unavailable, on a `line` disc while Kkachi uses it, teal while listening. A tap opens settings
  - at the lower right, a log icon button with a small `text` dot when something is new. It opens a bottom sheet, 기록: what happened on this device, newest first, each a `muted` time and a sentence ("오후 3:02 까치가 사진을 찍었어요 (저장 안 함)", "전했어요", "잘 못 들었어요. 다시 말해 주세요"), the last 50, in memory. The lines that used to sit on the screen (how to hold the talk pad, and the last thing Kkachi did) are gone from it: how to use a pad is its label, and what happened is in the log
  - a phone on its side keeps the button at the centre, with the words on its left and the status and inputs on its right, the input pill turned upright there (its icons one under another)
  - before the device is ready (naming, the start tap, waiting to be allowed), only the message and its button, in the middle
  - 음성 (on unless switched off): hold the pad to talk; it goes teal with `accent-ink` and a voice-level halo only while held, since that is listening, and how each thing said went is in the log. 버튼: press the pad, which shrinks to 96% while pressed. 다이얼: a round `bg` knob in the pad with a 4px `text` mark, turned by dragging around it through three quarters of a circle (0 at the lower left, 100 at the lower right, no easing: it follows the finger), its value beside the title. What the last two send goes wherever Kkachi Home has joined it. With all three off, one line says so
  - 이 기기, a bottom sheet opened from the settings icon or the input pill: cards in a grid that show how things are and switch nothing (what is open to Kkachi and what is joined to what is chosen in Kkachi Home only), in two sections: 출력 (까치가 이 기기로): 말하기, 조명, and 입력 (이 기기가 까치에게): 음성, 다이얼, 버튼, 사진, 소리 크기, 가속도·기울기, 위치. The inputs are named for what they are: 음성 (the hold-to-talk button, once 말 걸기) and 가속도·기울기 (the accelerometer, the tilt and the compass, once just 센서). Each card has a line icon, a name, a state (켜짐 / 꺼 둠 / 탭 필요 / 권한 없음 / 지원 안 함, or what it is doing now: 말하는 중, 찍는 중, 재는 중, 비추는 중) and an on/off switch. The switch track is `text` when on; off dims the icon and name; an unusable card loses its fill, keeps a `line` outline, goes `muted` and shows a not-allowed cursor. A card darkens to `line` while pressed. A card in use gets the `line` background, or teal while listening (재는 중, 듣는 중)

  Teal appears nowhere else on the page. As a lamp (`device_light`, colour and brightness chosen by Kkachi) the whole screen is that one color, dimmed by a `dim` layer, with no button on it; a tap turns it off. An outdated page shows a single "새 버전이 있어요. 눌러서 새로고침" button. Status lines are sentences, never joined with middle dots.
- **Device talk settings**: on the agent screen's 자원, a "공기계 대화" section of segmented controls, each with a small `muted` name above it (not tracked, not caps): 대화, 답하는 곳, 개인적인 내용, and, only when the devices have their own session, 도구 and 조용하면 새 대화 (four segments). One note below says what the current choice means.
- **Feed bubbles**: a device conversation shows in the transcript like any turn, with "거실에서" in `muted` small type right-aligned above your bubble; a message Kkachi sent to the phone is a Kkachi bubble alone, with "까치가 보냈어요" left-aligned above it.
- **Device photos**: a photo Kkachi took through a device appears in its bubble under the words, full width (max 360px tall).
- **Empty conversation**: nothing but the composer. Example sentences were rejected as clutter.
- **States**: mic idle / recording (teal with `accent-ink`, level halo) / thinking (dimmed) / speaking (bars) / disabled (mic permission denied). Icon buttons and settings controls have hover (pointer devices), active, focus-visible (3px teal ring, instant). Disabled is never opacity alone: send goes `line` with a `muted` arrow and a not-allowed cursor; a button waiting on the server goes `muted` with a progress cursor.
- **Keyboard**: Enter sends, Shift+Enter is a new line; outside the text box, hold Space to dictate.
- **Browser surfaces**: selection, caret, and scrollbar use the palette.
- **Dark mode**: designed per token, follows the system; `?theme=light` forces light for review.
- **Docs images** (`docs/banner.svg`, `docs/architecture-simple.svg`, `docs/architecture.svg`): the same tokens in their light values, SUIT, radius 14px or less on cards and pills for tags. The README header is `docs/banner.png`: the icon, the name, one line and three lines of what it does on the left; on the right, on `surface`, two cards: one turn as Kkachi Home shows it (your bubble, Kkachi's outlined reply, a teal 기억함 chip) and under it one connection as the 연결 tab draws it (거실 음성, its wire, 에이전트, a switch that is on). Its source `docs/banner.svg` links the icon and SUIT from outside, which GitHub does not load in an image, so render it to PNG after a change: `chrome --headless=new --allow-file-access-from-files --force-device-scale-factor=2 --window-size=1280,400 --virtual-time-budget=8000 --screenshot=docs/banner.png docs/banner.svg`. Diagrams read top to bottom from the clients to the services, teal for core, and dashed lines for core-only or optional links.
