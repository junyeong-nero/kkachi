# DESIGN.md: Kkachi web app

Single source of truth for the visual system of Kkachi Home (까치 홈, the app on the user's phone: `src/web/index.html`) and of the `/device` page. Interaction design (voice flow, states, latency) lives in [docs/design.md](docs/design.md).

## Discovery

- **Artifact**: mobile AI/conversational interface, text-first with optional dictation and read-aloud (Operate mode). Used one-handed on a phone.
- **Who / job**: one person talking to their own assistant. Primary action: type (or dictate) something, check it, send.
- **Brief**: clean and neutral like other AI products; the magpie (까치) appears only as the app icon and logo, the way Kitsune uses its fox. An earlier full-screen magpie illustration was rejected as kitschy.
- **Adjectives**: calm, clear, quick, trustworthy. Essence: *quiet text assistant*.

## Commitments

- **Aesthetic**: plain neutral surfaces, one accent, content first. Familiar chat patterns (your words right, Kkachi's answer left) because Operate mode rewards familiarity.
- **Signature move**: the mic button that draws your live voice level as a halo while recording, and "remembered" chips in the same magpie-wing teal. Teal only ever means "Kkachi is listening" or "Kkachi remembered this".
- **Symbol**: flat magpie head-and-shoulders mark (black, white belly and wing, one teal wing patch) on pale mint `#CEE8E2`, generated with Codex image generation on deep ink `#16181C` and moved onto mint by `assets/icon/recolor.py` (the black bird disappeared on the ink at home-screen size). Used for the app icon and the header logo only. Source and the two unused candidates (perched profile on off-white, in flight on pale teal) are in `assets/icon/`; `src/web/icon.png` (512), `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `favicon.png` (64) are resized from `icon-b-mint.png`.

- **Type**: SUIT Variable (Korean-native sans; not Inter/Pretendard by reflex). Scale ratio 1.2 from 16px: 13 / 16 / 19 / 23. Korean line breaks with `word-break: keep-all`.
- **Radius**: 8px (chips, small controls), 14px (bubbles, groups, sheet), pill (buttons, mic).
- **Shadow**: one soft elevation `0 1px 2px` in light mode only; dark mode uses lightness steps.
- **Motion**: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`, 120ms / 200ms. Only transform, opacity, color. Reduced motion keeps color and opacity changes, drops movement. The voice-level halo is a disc behind the mic scaled with transform, never an animated box-shadow, so focus rings appear at once.
- **What moves** (Kkachi Home; asked for, and added with impeccable's animate and the deslop motion reference): motion says where something came from or that a tap was taken, and nothing plays when the app opens. Reduced motion keeps every fade and drops every movement.
  - The menu slides in from the left (280ms, an iOS-sheet curve) over a scrim that fades in; dragged, it follows the finger with no easing, and on letting go it opens or closes by how far and how fast it went. A page opened from the menu comes 12px from the right with a fade (260ms).
  - A switch moves where it is, its knob widening while held, and its row dims after it (200ms); the list is drawn again only once it has moved. Before, the list was redrawn at once and the switch was never seen to move.
  - The toast comes 8px down from the top edge as it fades in. The `/device` page is left as it was: it sits lit in a room all day.
- **Spacing**: mostly 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20 and 24px, with 3 to 9px insets inside dense controls (chips, segments, table cells). Hallmark's 4px scale is noted, not adopted: the 10 and 14px values set the bubble and card padding, and changing them is a layout change, not a polish.

## Craft decisions

- **Screens**: Kkachi Home is the agent that stays home, called from anywhere: the conversation is the screen, and everything else is in a menu that slides in over it from the left, as the menus of Claude's and ChatGPT's apps do, so nothing sits under the composer. The menu opens with the button at the top left (three lines) or a swipe from the left edge of the screen (the first 24px), and closes with a swipe back, a tap on the scrim, or Escape. It is `bg`, at most 340px or 86% of the screen, with a shadow on its edge (a hairline in dark mode) over a `scrim` (36% in light, 55% in dark); while it is open the rest of the app is inert and focus is on the menu. At its top a small two-way segmented control of icons, 56px each, at its left edge: a phone for 기기 and a speech bubble for 대화, named for screen readers and on hover (대화 first; the one chosen is remembered on this device). 대화 has 새 대화 (a row with its icon) and under it 지난 대화, the conversations newest first (Pi's session files, `src/conversations.ts`): the first thing said, two lines at most, and when it last went on; the one going on now says 지금 대화 and is semibold. One tapped is opened again (Pi switches to it) and shown in the transcript. 기기 has 기기 추가 (a row with a plus) and the devices as a grouped list. Kept at the bottom over a hairline, under both: 설정, with a gear. A device, 기기 추가 and 설정 open as a page over the conversation with a `muted` back button ("‹ 메뉴") above the heading; back shows the menu again, where they were opened from. Every page starts with a 23px heading and at most one `muted` paragraph; no label sits above a heading. The top bar has the menu button and, at its right end, whether home answers: a `muted` pill with a light, 집, 기기 N대 켜짐 (or 집 alone with no devices), and 집에 연결할 수 없어요 in `danger` with a red light when the server does not answer. It is read when the app opens, when it comes back to the front and every 30 seconds, and opens the menu on 기기. There is no bar at the bottom. The conversation going on is shown again when the app opens (iOS often closes a home-screen app). With nothing said yet the transcript shows, in its middle, the app's icon (56px), 집에 있는 까치예요 (19px, bold) and one `muted` line on what to ask. Before this there were three tabs in a bar floating over the bottom (대화, 기기, 설정), and five before that (입력, 출력, 연결, 에이전트, 설정): the bar sat right where the thumb reaches for the text box, and the conversation is the thing used most.
- **Devices (기기)**: in the menu, one grouped list of the spare phones and tablets (this app is not among them: it is where they are looked after). A row is a 32px round `bg` picture of what the device is (a phone, a tablet), its name, its state in `muted` (켜져 있어요, 화면을 한 번 눌러야 해요, 꺼져 있어요), a light and a chevron; the whole row opens the device's page and darkens to `line` while pressed. The light repeats the words for the eye and is hidden from screen readers: green when it can be used, a hollow green ring when it is on but waits for a tap on its screen, grey when off. A device that is off has its name and picture `muted` and regular weight. One waiting to be allowed says 허용을 기다려요 and has a filled 허용 pill in place of its light. The list is read again every 5 seconds while the menu or a device's page is open.
  - 기기 추가 (the plus): the address to open on the spare phone on a `surface` band with a 복사 pill (it says 복사했어요 for a moment; with no clipboard, the address is selected and a toast says to press and hold it), then four numbered steps, each a title and a `muted` line: 이 주소를 여세요 (in the phone's Safari or Chrome, signed in to the same Tailscale account), 이름을 붙이고 화면을 한 번 누르세요, 여기서 허용하세요, 화면을 켜 두세요. Numbered because they are a sequence.
  - A device's page: its state as a sentence, then **지금 이 방**, what is happening in that room, read when it opens (POST `/api/device/<id>/read`) with a refresh button at the end of the heading: 밝기 (a sun: 어두워요. 불이 꺼진 것 같아요, 어둑해요, 불이 켜진 방 정도로 밝아요, 밝아요, 햇빛처럼 아주 밝아요, with 약 N lx when the camera gives its exposure), 소리 (조용해요, 보통이에요, 시끄러워요, and 가끔 큰 소리가 나요 when the loudest moment stood out; no decibels), 움직임 (when it last moved), 놓인 자세 (how it lies, and the way it faces), 위치 (알아요 and how close, with a 지도 pill that opens the place on a map: not numbers), each a row with its picture and words, 재는 중… or 읽는 중… until they come; and 사진, a row whose 찍기 pill takes one with the back camera and shows it under the group, full width (max 360px tall). Opening a device never takes a photo by itself. One note says the device's own log records that Kkachi Home read it. Then **까치가 쓸 수 있는 것**: one row per part (음성, 스피커, 조명 on a tablet, 카메라, 소리 크기, 움직임 센서, 위치), each its picture, its name, why it cannot be used now when it cannot (기기 화면을 한 번 눌러야 써요, 이 기기에서 쓸 수 없어요), and a switch. A switch that is on for a part that cannot work now has a dimmed track: open to Kkachi, but not working. This is the only place where something is opened to Kkachi or closed. Last, 이 기기 삭제, a `surface` button in `danger`, which asks first. A device that is off shows only its parts and the delete button.
- **Lights**: whether a device can be used is one 10px dot in its list row: `ok` green it is on, grey (`muted` at 45%) it is off. The words stay for screen readers (the dot's label). Green here is a state, not the accent: teal still means only listening and remembered.
- **Edges**: each side of a screen has two lines. The screen's own, 20px in: a heading starts on it, a group fills from one to the other, and what is drawn in the icon button at the end of a heading (refresh, the read-again button of a room) stops on it, the button itself running 14px past. A group's own, 14px inside its surface: a row's picture or first word starts on it, and the control that ends a row stops on it, the same on every screen (a switch's track, the 찍기, 허용 and 복사 pills, the light of a device in the list). A control is at least 44px to tap and that area may run past the line: it is what is seen that lines up. Checked against Material 3 (Lists: one position for each part of a row, controls in the trailing slot, 16dp before it; Switch: it acts at once and sits beside its label), Apple's guidelines (a switch belongs in a list row, whose words are its label; a destructive action asks first) and WCAG 2.2 (target size). The rules are taken from them, not the sizes: the switch (38 by 22px) and the 14px inset are this app's own, and the 44px target is Apple's (Material asks for 48dp).
- **Conversation layout**: transcript / composer. The composer is one `surface` card (24px radius, a `line` border that turns `accent` while typing): the text box across its whole width, and under it a row of 44px buttons, `(photo) ……… (mic) (send)`, photo and mic without a fill or border of their own. The card grows upwards as one piece as the words get longer (up to 200px of text, then it scrolls), so the buttons stay with it; before, the buttons sat beside a narrow text box that grew alone. A one-line status sits above the card only when there is something to say only when there is something to say (recording, transcribing, an error). Picked or taken photos (up to 4, scaled to 1600px JPEG on the phone) wait as 64px thumbnails with a remove button inside the card, above the words, and appear above your words in your bubble once sent. A photo alone can be sent. The mic is tap to start / tap to stop, or hold and let go; transcribed words are appended to the text box to check and fix, and nothing is sent until you press send (or Enter).
- **Transcript**: last 30 turns of this page, as chat bubbles: yours a light `surface` fill on the right, Kkachi's outlined (`bg` with a `line` border) on the left (fill vs outline keeps them apart without heavy color) with the working dots and activity label inside it. Kkachi's bubble appears once your words are known and is removed if the turn ends without words. Replies are Markdown (marked + DOMPurify, vendored in `src/web/vendor/`); narration before a tool call stays in the bubble as a muted aside. Read-aloud is off by default (per device) and speaks only the first two sentences.
- **Settings**: first a grouped list of categories, each row an icon, its name, what it is set to now in `muted`, and a chevron: 계정 (two groups under small headings, AI and 서비스; 서비스 lists Google, Notion and GitHub, each with a 연결 pill opening its form in the group, then one row per server it added, with a filled 로그인 pill while it wants a sign-in and 연결 해제; signing in opens a `surface` panel under the group with the page to open and a field for the address the browser ended on; the model accounts: one row each with its state and a 연결 or 연결 해제 pill; connecting opens a `surface` panel under the list with the steps, numbered lines in `muted`, a filled pill for the page to open and a field for the code; ChatGPT's device code shows at 23px, bold, selectable), 외형 (화면: 폰 설정대로, 밝게 or 어둡게; 글자 크기; and 대화, two switch rows kept on this phone, 생각 과정 보기 and 도구 사용 보기: in each reply, in the order they came and in `muted` `t-sm`, the model's thinking as a `생각` disclosure (open while it is written, folded once the answer begins; its text behind a `line` rule on the left) and each tool call as one line, …, ✓ or ✕ in `danger`, its name and its arguments in monospace, cut to one line), 음성 (two groups: 목소리, for when Kkachi speaks: 이 폰에서 읽어 주기 with a switch, 목소리 showing the engine and voice and opening a page of its own (기기 | 이 컴퓨터 | 온라인 as a segmented control, a line on whether words leave the house, then that engine's voices with 들어보기; each engine keeps the voice last chosen in it), and 말 빠르기 as a slider row; 받아쓰기, for when you speak: 받아쓰는 곳 and 다듬기, each a row with a small two-way segment and a short `muted` state under its name, "밖으로 안 나가요" or "Groq 로 나가요", in `danger` when that way is not ready), 모델 (the model and 생각 깊이), 도구. Under them, in a group of its own, 앱 다시 불러오기 (a home-screen app has no reload). One opened is a page of its own with a `muted` back button ("‹ 설정") and its 23px name, its settings as grouped rows and segmented controls under small `muted` headings. 도구 has 기본 (Kkachi's own servers, no controls), 추가한 것 (each with a bin icon button and a switch; a server that failed says why in `danger`) and 추가하기 (the suggestions with an 추가 pill, opening a form in the group: fields with small `muted` labels over 44px inputs on `bg`, a link to where the value comes from, a filled 추가 pill and 취소), then 직접 추가 (주소 or 명령 as a segmented control). Pi is not named anywhere in the app. 새 대화 and the conversations are in the menu.
- **`/device` page**: dark only, because it sits in a room all day. The main screen is for talking to Kkachi; its sheet shows what is open to Kkachi and switches nothing, and the lamp has no controls here (Kkachi picks its colour). The page always fits the screen and never scrolls: the button scales with the height (80 to 136px) and heard and said are cut to a few lines. The talk button sits at the exact centre of the screen: top and bottom padding are equal, and the rows above and below it are equal.
  - a floating bar with the logo and the device name (22px), and a settings icon button on the right
  - at the centre, the talk pad, while this device's voice is open to Kkachi: a square (radius 14px, `surface` with a `line` border, 96 to 164px by the screen) with its line icon above a 13px `muted` title. With the voice closed, one line says so and where to open it
  - above the button, what was heard in quotes (`muted`) and the words spoken (current ones in `text`, earlier ones `muted`), sitting just over it
  - below the pads, one status line; at the bottom, the input environment: a pill with one icon per input (음성, 카메라, 소리 크기, 움직임 센서, 위치), dimmed when off or unavailable, on a `line` disc while Kkachi uses it, teal while listening. A tap opens settings
  - at the lower right, a log icon button with a small `text` dot when something is new. It opens a bottom sheet, 기록: what happened on this device, newest first, each a `muted` time and a sentence ("오후 3:02 까치가 사진을 찍었어요 (저장 안 함)", "전했어요", "잘 못 들었어요. 다시 말해 주세요"), the last 50, in memory. The lines that used to sit on the screen (how to hold the talk pad, and the last thing Kkachi did) are gone from it: how to use a pad is its label, and what happened is in the log
  - a phone on its side keeps the button at the centre, with the words on its left and the status and inputs on its right, the input pill turned upright there (its icons one under another)
  - before the device is ready (naming, the start tap, waiting to be allowed), only the message and its button, in the middle
  - 음성: hold the pad to talk; it goes teal with `accent-ink` and a voice-level halo only while held, since that is listening, and how each thing said went is in the log. The answer comes a sentence at a time, each said as it arrives. A button and a dial were pads here too, joined to other devices' lamps; they went with the connections
  - 이 기기, a bottom sheet opened from the settings icon or the input pill: cards in a grid that show how things are and switch nothing (what is open to Kkachi is chosen in Kkachi Home only), in two sections: 출력 (까치가 이 기기로): 말하기, 조명, and 입력 (이 기기가 까치에게): 음성, 사진, 소리 크기, 가속도·기울기, 위치. The inputs are named for what they are: 음성 (the hold-to-talk button, once 말 걸기) and 가속도·기울기 (the accelerometer, the tilt and the compass, once just 센서). Each card has a line icon, a name, a state (켜짐 / 꺼 둠 / 탭 필요 / 권한 없음 / 지원 안 함, or what it is doing now: 말하는 중, 찍는 중, 재는 중, 비추는 중) and an on/off switch. The switch track is `text` when on; off dims the icon and name; an unusable card loses its fill, keeps a `line` outline, goes `muted` and shows a not-allowed cursor. A card darkens to `line` while pressed. A card in use gets the `line` background, or teal while listening (재는 중, 듣는 중)

  Teal appears nowhere else on the page. As a lamp (`device_light`, colour and brightness chosen by Kkachi) the whole screen is that one color, dimmed by a `dim` layer, with no button on it; a tap turns it off. An outdated page shows a single "새 버전이 있어요. 눌러서 새로고침" button. Status lines are sentences, never joined with middle dots.
- **Device talk**: no settings. What is said to a device is a turn of Kkachi Home's conversation with its tools, the device that heard it answers, and Kkachi Home shows the turn without reading it aloud (docs/bus.md).
- **Feed bubbles**: a device conversation shows in the transcript like any turn, with "거실에서" in `muted` small type right-aligned above your bubble; a message Kkachi sent to the phone is a Kkachi bubble alone, with "까치가 보냈어요" left-aligned above it.
- **Device photos**: a photo Kkachi took through a device appears in its bubble under the words, full width (max 360px tall).
- **Empty conversation**: the app icon, 집에 있는 까치예요 and one line on what to ask, in the middle; then the composer. Example sentences were rejected as clutter.
- **Raised and scrim**: `raised` is the chosen part of a segmented control (`bg` in light; in dark a step lighter than its `surface` track, so it reads as above it, not pressed in). `scrim` is under the menu. 외형's 화면 can fix light or dark on this device (`data-theme`), whatever the phone says.
- **Touch and zoom**: pinch zoom is the reader's (no `user-scalable=no`); double-tap zoom is off (`touch-action: manipulation`). Everything pressed is 44px at least, also segment labels and the photo's remove button (a 22px disc with a 44px area).
- **States**: mic idle / recording (teal with `accent-ink`, level halo) / thinking (dimmed) / speaking (bars) / disabled (mic permission denied). Icon buttons and settings controls have hover (pointer devices), active, focus-visible (3px teal ring, instant). Disabled is never opacity alone: send goes `line` with a `muted` arrow and a not-allowed cursor; a button waiting on the server goes `muted` with a progress cursor.
- **Keyboard**: Enter sends, Shift+Enter is a new line; outside the text box, hold Space to dictate.
- **Browser surfaces**: selection, caret, and scrollbar use the palette.
- **Dark mode**: designed per token, follows the system; `?theme=light` forces light for review.
- **Docs images** (`docs/banner-light.svg`, `docs/banner-dark.svg`, `docs/architecture-simple.svg`, `docs/architecture.svg`): the same tokens in their light values, SUIT, radius 14px or less on cards and pills for tags. The README header is the banner, as a `<picture>` that follows GitHub's theme: on a transparent background, the app icon (its mint tile, 200px with a 46px radius: the magpie's black body would vanish on a dark page without it) and the name, Kkachi, in SUIT 800 at 196px, `text` (#161b20) in `banner-light.png` and the dark `text` (#f0f3f5) in `banner-dark.png`. The line "An agent that stays home: call it from anywhere." is centred text under it, not in the image. What the product looks like is not in the banner: it is `docs/demo.gif` under the first paragraph, recorded from Kkachi Home with sample devices on a tablet (768 by 1024px). The banner sources link the icon and SUIT from outside, which GitHub does not load in an image, so render them to PNG after a change, each with its own name: `chrome --headless=new --allow-file-access-from-files --force-device-scale-factor=2 --window-size=960,240 --default-background-color=00000000 --virtual-time-budget=8000 --screenshot=docs/banner-light.png docs/banner-light.svg`. Diagrams read top to bottom from the clients to the services, teal for core, and dashed lines for core-only or optional links.
