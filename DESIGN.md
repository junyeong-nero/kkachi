# DESIGN.md: Kkachi web app

Single source of truth for the visual system of Kkachi Home (까치 홈, the app on the user's phone: `src/web/index.html`) and of the `/device` page. Interaction design (voice flow, states, latency) lives in [docs/design.md](docs/design.md).

## Discovery

- **Artifact**: mobile AI/conversational interface, text-first with optional dictation and read-aloud (Operate mode). Used one-handed on a phone.
- **Who / job**: one person talking to their own assistant. Primary action: type (or dictate) something, check it, send.
- **Brief**: clean and neutral like other AI products; the magpie (까치) appears only as the app icon and logo, the way Kitsune uses its fox. An earlier full-screen magpie illustration was rejected as kitschy.
- **Adjectives**: calm, clear, quick, trustworthy. Essence: *quiet text assistant*.

## Commitments

- **Aesthetic**: plain neutral surfaces, one accent, content first. Familiar chat patterns (your words right, Kkachi's answer left) because Operate mode rewards familiarity.
- **Signature move**: the mic button that draws your live voice level as a halo while recording, and "remembered" chips in the same magpie-wing teal. Teal only ever means "Kkachi is listening" or "Kkachi remembered this".
- **Symbol**: flat magpie head-and-shoulders mark (black, white belly and wing, one teal wing patch) on pale mint `#CEE8E2`, generated with Codex image generation on deep ink `#16181C` and moved onto mint by `assets/icon/recolor.py` (the black bird disappeared on the ink at home-screen size). Used for the app icon and the header logo only. Source and the two unused candidates (perched profile on off-white, in flight on pale teal) are in `assets/icon/`; `src/web/icon.png` (512), `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `favicon.png` (64) are resized from `icon-b-mint.png`.

- **Type**: SUIT Variable (Korean-native sans; not Inter/Pretendard by reflex). Scale ratio 1.2 from 16px: 13 / 16 / 19 / 23. Korean line breaks with `word-break: keep-all`.
- **Radius**: 8px (chips, small controls), 14px (bubbles, groups, sheet), pill (buttons, mic).
- **Shadow**: one soft elevation `0 1px 2px` in light mode only; dark mode uses lightness steps.
- **Motion**: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`, 120ms / 200ms. Only transform, opacity, color. Reduced motion keeps color and opacity changes, drops movement. The voice-level halo is a disc behind the mic scaled with transform, never an animated box-shadow, so focus rings appear at once.
- **What moves** (Kkachi Home; asked for, and added with impeccable's animate and the deslop motion reference): motion says where something came from or that a tap was taken, and nothing plays when the app opens. Reduced motion keeps every fade and drops every movement.
  - The bar's pill is one thing that slides from tab to tab (260ms); the screen arrived at fades in (200ms). A device opened from the list also comes 12px from the right, and going back from the left (260ms).
  - A switch moves where it is, its knob widening while held, and its row dims after it (200ms); the list is drawn again only once it has moved. Before, the list was redrawn at once and the switch was never seen to move.
  - The toast comes 8px down from the top edge as it fades in. The `/device` page is left as it was: it sits lit in a room all day.
- **Spacing**: mostly 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20 and 24px, with 3 to 9px insets inside dense controls (chips, segments, table cells). Hallmark's 4px scale is noted, not adopted: the 10 and 14px values set the bubble and card padding, and changing them is a layout change, not a polish.

## Craft decisions

- **Screens**: Kkachi Home is the agent that stays home, called from anywhere: you talk to it, and you look into the rooms it can see and choose what it may use there. A bar floating over the bottom of the screen switches three screens: 대화, 기기, 설정. It is a translucent pill, asked for in the style of Apple's floating tab bars: `bg` at 72% over a 20px backdrop blur, at most 320px wide, 12px above the bottom edge (or the home-indicator inset), lifted by a two-layer shadow in light mode and a hairline in dark mode. The screens scroll behind it and end with room to clear it. Each tab is a 22px line icon over a 12px label, `muted`; the current one is `text` and bold on a 9% `text` pill (no teal: it is not listening). 대화 opens first. The bar goes away while typing (the on-screen keyboard is up, or iOS's pill for a joined keyboard). There is no header: the screens start under the status bar, and the logo is the app icon only. Refresh is an icon button at the right end of the settings screen's heading. Nothing is a popup: settings is a screen, and a device opens in place with a `muted` back button ("‹ 기기") above its heading; tapping the current tab again also goes back. Every screen starts with a 23px heading and at most one `muted` paragraph; no label sits above a heading. Five screens came before (입력, 출력, 연결, 에이전트, 설정): with the connections gone (devices joined to each other with no agent, now left to Home Assistant), 입력, 출력 and the agent's 자원 were one thing seen three ways, and became 기기.
- **Devices (기기)**: one grouped list of the spare phones and tablets (this app is not among them: it is where they are looked after). A row is a 36px round `bg` picture of what the device is (a phone, a tablet), its name, how it is in `muted` (켜져 있어요, 꺼져 있어요, 허용을 기다려요), a light and a chevron; the whole row opens the device and darkens to `line` while pressed. A device that is off has its name and picture `muted` and regular weight. One waiting to be allowed has a filled 허용 pill in place of its light and chevron. Under the list, one note says how to add one (the `/device` address). The list is read again every 5 seconds while it shows.
  - A device opened: its state as a sentence, then **지금 이 방**, what is happening in that room, read when it opens (POST `/api/device/<id>/read`) with a refresh button at the end of the heading: 소리 (조용해요, 보통이에요, 시끄러워요, with the levels), 움직임 (when it last moved), 놓인 자세 (how it lies, and the way it faces), 위치, each a row with its picture and words, 재는 중… or 읽는 중… until they come; and 사진, a row whose 찍기 pill takes one with the back camera and shows it under the group, full width (max 360px tall). Opening a device never takes a photo by itself. One note says the device's own log records that Kkachi Home read it. Then **까치가 쓸 수 있는 것**: one row per part (음성, 스피커, 조명 on a tablet, 카메라, 소리 크기, 가속도 센서, 위치), each its picture, its name, why it cannot be used now when it cannot (기기 화면을 한 번 눌러야 써요, 이 기기에서 쓸 수 없어요), and a switch. This is the only place where something is opened to Kkachi or closed. Last, 이 기기 삭제, a `surface` button in `danger`, which asks first. A device that is off shows only its parts and the delete button.
- **Lights**: whether a device can be used is one 10px dot in its list row: `ok` green it is on, grey (`muted` at 45%) it is off. The words stay for screen readers (the dot's label). Green here is a state, not the accent: teal still means only listening and remembered.
- **Edges**: each side of a screen has two lines. The screen's own, 20px in: a heading starts on it, a group fills from one to the other, and what is drawn in the icon button at the end of a heading (the plus, refresh) stops on it, the button itself running 14px past. A group's own, 14px inside its surface: a row's picture or first word starts on it, and the control that ends a row stops on it, the same on every screen (a switch's track, the 찍기 and 허용 pills, the chevron of a device in the list). A control is at least 44px to tap and that area may run past the line: it is what is seen that lines up. Checked against Material 3 (Lists: one position for each part of a row, controls in the trailing slot, 16dp before it; Switch: it acts at once and sits beside its label), Apple's guidelines (a switch belongs in a list row, whose words are its label; a destructive action asks first) and WCAG 2.2 (target size). The rules are taken from them, not the sizes: the switch (38 by 22px) and the 14px inset are this app's own, and the 44px target is Apple's (Material asks for 48dp).
- **Conversation layout**: transcript / composer: `(photo) [text box] (mic) (send)`, with a one-line status above it only when there is something to say (recording, transcribing, an error). Picked or taken photos (up to 4, scaled to 1600px JPEG on the phone) wait as 64px thumbnails with a remove button above the text box, and appear above your words in your bubble once sent. A photo alone can be sent. The mic is tap to start / tap to stop, or hold and let go; transcribed words are appended to the text box to check and fix, and nothing is sent until you press send (or Enter).
- **Transcript**: last 30 turns of this page, as chat bubbles: yours a light `surface` fill on the right, Kkachi's outlined (`bg` with a `line` border) on the left (fill vs outline keeps them apart without heavy color) with the working dots and activity label inside it. Kkachi's bubble appears once your words are known and is removed if the turn ends without words. Replies are Markdown (marked + DOMPurify, vendored in `src/web/vendor/`); narration before a tool call stays in the bubble as a muted aside. Read-aloud is off by default (per device) and speaks only the first two sentences.
- **Settings**: a screen of grouped rows and segmented controls under small `muted` headings: 읽어 주기 (and 목소리, 말 빠르기), 글자 크기, 생각 깊이, 모델, 공기계에 한 말 (how Kkachi answers what is said to a device), 까치의 MCP 서버 (the servers in `kkachi.mcp.json`, each "연결됨. 도구 N개" or "응답이 없어요" in `danger`), Pi 에 등록한 MCP 서버 ("등록됨": Pi connects to them itself, so no live state), 까치를 쓴 기록 (the last 30 turns from Kkachi Home and the devices: the words, two lines at most, then when, where from and how it went as sentences, "오전 11:04, 까치 홈. 6.8초 걸렸어요. 도구를 1번 썼어요", a failed one in `danger`), and 새 대화 시작.
- **`/device` page**: dark only, because it sits in a room all day. The main screen is for talking to Kkachi; its sheet shows what is open to Kkachi and switches nothing, and the lamp has no controls here (Kkachi picks its colour). The page always fits the screen and never scrolls: the button scales with the height (80 to 136px) and heard and said are cut to a few lines. The talk button sits at the exact centre of the screen: top and bottom padding are equal, and the rows above and below it are equal.
  - a floating bar with the logo and the device name (22px), and a settings icon button on the right
  - at the centre, the talk pad, while this device's voice is open to Kkachi: a square (radius 14px, `surface` with a `line` border, 96 to 164px by the screen) with its line icon above a 13px `muted` title. With the voice closed, one line says so and where to open it
  - above the button, what was heard in quotes (`muted`) and the words spoken (current ones in `text`, earlier ones `muted`), sitting just over it
  - below the pads, one status line; at the bottom, the input environment: a pill with one icon per input (음성, 카메라, 소리 크기, 가속도 센서, 위치), dimmed when off or unavailable, on a `line` disc while Kkachi uses it, teal while listening. A tap opens settings
  - at the lower right, a log icon button with a small `text` dot when something is new. It opens a bottom sheet, 기록: what happened on this device, newest first, each a `muted` time and a sentence ("오후 3:02 까치가 사진을 찍었어요 (저장 안 함)", "전했어요", "잘 못 들었어요. 다시 말해 주세요"), the last 50, in memory. The lines that used to sit on the screen (how to hold the talk pad, and the last thing Kkachi did) are gone from it: how to use a pad is its label, and what happened is in the log
  - a phone on its side keeps the button at the centre, with the words on its left and the status and inputs on its right, the input pill turned upright there (its icons one under another)
  - before the device is ready (naming, the start tap, waiting to be allowed), only the message and its button, in the middle
  - 음성: hold the pad to talk; it goes teal with `accent-ink` and a voice-level halo only while held, since that is listening, and how each thing said went is in the log. The answer comes a sentence at a time, each said as it arrives. A button and a dial were pads here too, joined to other devices' lamps; they went with the connections
  - 이 기기, a bottom sheet opened from the settings icon or the input pill: cards in a grid that show how things are and switch nothing (what is open to Kkachi is chosen in Kkachi Home only), in two sections: 출력 (까치가 이 기기로): 말하기, 조명, and 입력 (이 기기가 까치에게): 음성, 사진, 소리 크기, 가속도·기울기, 위치. The inputs are named for what they are: 음성 (the hold-to-talk button, once 말 걸기) and 가속도·기울기 (the accelerometer, the tilt and the compass, once just 센서). Each card has a line icon, a name, a state (켜짐 / 꺼 둠 / 탭 필요 / 권한 없음 / 지원 안 함, or what it is doing now: 말하는 중, 찍는 중, 재는 중, 비추는 중) and an on/off switch. The switch track is `text` when on; off dims the icon and name; an unusable card loses its fill, keeps a `line` outline, goes `muted` and shows a not-allowed cursor. A card darkens to `line` while pressed. A card in use gets the `line` background, or teal while listening (재는 중, 듣는 중)

  Teal appears nowhere else on the page. As a lamp (`device_light`, colour and brightness chosen by Kkachi) the whole screen is that one color, dimmed by a `dim` layer, with no button on it; a tap turns it off. An outdated page shows a single "새 버전이 있어요. 눌러서 새로고침" button. Status lines are sentences, never joined with middle dots.
- **Device talk settings**: on 설정, a "공기계에 한 말" section of segmented controls, each with a small `muted` name above it (not tracked, not caps): 대화, 답하는 곳, 개인적인 내용, 내 MCP, and, only when the devices have their own session, 도구 and 조용하면 새 대화 (four segments). One note below says what the current choice means.
- **Feed bubbles**: a device conversation shows in the transcript like any turn, with "거실에서" in `muted` small type right-aligned above your bubble; a message Kkachi sent to the phone is a Kkachi bubble alone, with "까치가 보냈어요" left-aligned above it.
- **Device photos**: a photo Kkachi took through a device appears in its bubble under the words, full width (max 360px tall).
- **Empty conversation**: nothing but the composer. Example sentences were rejected as clutter.
- **States**: mic idle / recording (teal with `accent-ink`, level halo) / thinking (dimmed) / speaking (bars) / disabled (mic permission denied). Icon buttons and settings controls have hover (pointer devices), active, focus-visible (3px teal ring, instant). Disabled is never opacity alone: send goes `line` with a `muted` arrow and a not-allowed cursor; a button waiting on the server goes `muted` with a progress cursor.
- **Keyboard**: Enter sends, Shift+Enter is a new line; outside the text box, hold Space to dictate.
- **Browser surfaces**: selection, caret, and scrollbar use the palette.
- **Dark mode**: designed per token, follows the system; `?theme=light` forces light for review.
- **Docs images** (`docs/banner.svg`, `docs/architecture-simple.svg`, `docs/architecture.svg`): the same tokens in their light values, SUIT, radius 14px or less on cards and pills for tags. The README header is `docs/banner.png`: centred on `surface`, the icon large (144px), the name, and one line ("An agent that stays home: call it from anywhere."). What the product looks like is not in the banner: it is `docs/demo.gif` under the first paragraph, recorded from Kkachi Home with sample devices (거실, 주방, 서재) at 390 by 760px. Its source `docs/banner.svg` links the icon and SUIT from outside, which GitHub does not load in an image, so render it to PNG after a change: `chrome --headless=new --allow-file-access-from-files --force-device-scale-factor=2 --window-size=1280,400 --virtual-time-budget=8000 --screenshot=docs/banner.png docs/banner.svg`. Diagrams read top to bottom from the clients to the services, teal for core, and dashed lines for core-only or optional links.
